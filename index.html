"Uhm, guys? What's that??? Uh oh, it's THE FELLANATOR! Your cloning machine (The Fellanator) has been transformed by an evil being, and now the machine is spawning EVIL VERSIONS OF YOU AND YOUR FRIENDS!!! WHATEVER WILL YOU DO!?!?! You and your gang of Fellas must destroy this machine and these demonic clones before they take over the world! You can use anything, from swords, to rocket launchers, to defeat The Inverted!


This is INVERSION. A cheap, indie, no-ai-used, game by me, Thechicken64! Inversion is a rougelite survival game about fighting against waves of evil clones that come to destroy your world. Inversion releases December 8th, 2026 for $2.49. The demo for Inversion is already out, is free, and will be updated for the full version.
